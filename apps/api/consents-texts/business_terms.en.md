# Terms for Organizations

## 1. Parties and definitions

1.1. **Operator** — {{legalName}}, BIN {{bin}}, address: {{address}}.

1.2. **Business user (the Organization)** — a legal entity or an individual entrepreneur for which an organization has been created on the platform (Article 29 of the Digital Code of the Republic of Kazakhstan).

1.3. **Owner** — a platform user who created the organization or received its ownership. The Owner confirms that they are entitled to act on behalf of the Organization and accepts these terms on its behalf.

1.4. The "Terms of Use" (for every person in the organization) and the "Data Processing Agreement" also apply.

## 2. Subject

The Operator gives the Organization access to the business functions of the platform: employees and structure, sites, documents and electronic document exchange, processes, HR documents, counterparties, the virtual office, API keys, bots and webhooks — within the chosen plan.

## 3. Employee accounts

3.1. An employee works under their personal platform account connected to the organization by a "work pass". The employee's personal data and personal space do not belong to the Organization.

3.2. The Organization itself defines the roles and rights of employees and is responsible for their actions in its space. On dismissal, access is terminated by the Organization.

## 4. The Organization's data

4.1. The data entered by the Organization and its employees in the workspace belongs to the Organization. The Operator processes it only to provide the services.

4.2. **Export.** The Organization may export its data at any time using the platform tools (files, documents, registers) and via the API.

4.3. **Backups.** The Operator performs backups. Restoring individual objects deleted by the Organization is possible within the recycle bin and the archive; after their periods expire it is not guaranteed.

4.4. **No copies of identity documents.** The Organization does not upload copies of identity documents to the platform, except in cases expressly provided by the laws of the Republic of Kazakhstan (paragraph 10 of Article 7 of the Law "On Personal Data and Their Protection"). The necessary details are entered as fields.

## 5. Plans and payment

5.1. The set of functions and the limits are determined by the plan. The plan terms are shown before payment; when a limit is exceeded, the function is restricted with an explanation rather than switched off silently.

5.2. A trial period is provided once. After the paid period ends, a grace period applies, then the organization is moved to the free plan; the data is not deleted.

## 6. Availability

6.1. The Operator strives to keep the platform available at least 99.5 % of the month, excluding scheduled maintenance announced in advance.

6.2. Scheduled maintenance is performed mainly at night, Astana time.

## 7. Liability between the parties

7.1. The parties act as entrepreneurs; consumer protection law does not apply to these terms.

7.2. The Operator's aggregate liability for a calendar year is limited to the amount paid by the Organization over the last 12 months. The Operator is not liable for lost profit or indirect losses. The limitation does not apply in case of intent or where the law prohibits limiting liability.

7.3. The Organization is responsible for the lawfulness of the data it enters, for obtaining the consents of its employees, customers and counterparties, and for the content of the documents it creates and signs on the platform.

7.4. The Organization is responsible for its webhook addresses and for keeping its API keys safe; actions performed with its key are deemed its actions.

## 8. Integrations and bots

API keys, bots and webhooks are used under the platform rules: request limits, scopes, no bypassing of restrictions. The Operator may suspend a key or a webhook address in case of a security threat, notifying the Organization.

## 9. Suspension and termination

9.1. The Operator may suspend the organization for a gross violation of these terms or the law, notifying the owner and stating the reason. During suspension the data is preserved and available for export.

9.2. The Organization may delete the organization at any time. The organization is placed in the archive for the established period (it can be restored), then the data is destroyed and the organization's encryption keys are destroyed irreversibly.

## 10. Changes to the terms

10.1. A new version is published no later than 30 days before it takes effect; the owner and the administrators receive a notification and see what has changed.

10.2. If the owner has not accepted the new version by the effective date, only the owner's management actions (settings, membership, plan, keys) are restricted. **Employees' work does not stop.** Accepting the version lifts the restriction at once.
