# Consent to the Collection and Processing of Personal Data

Given under Articles 7 and 8 of the Law of the Republic of Kazakhstan "On Personal Data and Their Protection".

## 1. Operator

{{legalName}}, BIN {{bin}}, address: {{address}} (the Operator). The person responsible for organising the processing of personal data is the {{dpoTitle}} of the Operator. Contact: {{privacyEmail}}.

## 2. Data subject

The data subject is the user who accepts this consent during registration. The surname, first name and patronymic (if any) stated in the account are entered into a personal "Consent sheet" available in the "My data" section of the profile. If the consent is given by a legal representative, both persons are stated in the sheet.

## 3. Purposes of processing

- creating and maintaining the account, signing in and protection against unauthorised access;
- operation of the "between people" services of the platform: tasks, calendar, messenger, finance, documents, shop, organizations;
- sending service messages and notifications;
- compliance with the laws of the Republic of Kazakhstan;
- improving the platform using anonymised data.

## 4. List of data collected

- phone number; surname, first name, patronymic; date of birth; profile photo;
- city, "about me", e-mail address, marital status, links to social networks — if you provided them;
- IIN, residential address, identity document details — if you filled in the "For contracts and employment" block;
- the content you create: messages, tasks, notes, files, documents, recordings;
- technical data: IP address, device and browser details, session identifiers, the device token for notifications;
- information about your actions on the platform and the consents you gave.

The Operator does not collect biometric data or copies of identity documents.

## 5. Actions with the data

Collection, recording, storage, modification, use, anonymisation, blocking and destruction — using automated means.

## 6. Term of the consent

The consent is valid from acceptance until the account is deleted. After that the Operator keeps only the data it must keep by law (for example, records of financial operations and signed documents) — for the periods set by law, with restricted access and other processing suspended.

## 7. Transfer to third parties

The Operator transfers data to third parties only to the extent necessary for the stated purposes:

- KazInfoTeh LLP (Republic of Kazakhstan) — the phone number and the message text, to send SMS;
- National Information Technologies JSC — the National Certification Authority of the Republic of Kazakhstan — certificate data and the IIN, to verify an electronic digital signature when you use one;
- the organization you joined on the platform — the information needed to work in it. Such an organization is an independent owner of the data about its employees;
- state bodies — in cases expressly provided by law.

The current list and the history of transfers of your own data are shown in the "My data" section.

## 8. Cross-border transfer

Cross-border transfer takes place only on the basis of a separate consent — the "Consent to the Cross-Border Transfer of Personal Data". The database containing personal data is stored on servers in the Republic of Kazakhstan.

## 9. Dissemination in public sources

The Operator does not publish your data on its own initiative. Only what you yourself decide to show becomes public: card fields according to your visibility settings, the ability to find you by phone number (the first name and the first letter of the surname are shown), outbound links you create. The settings can be changed in the profile at any time.

## 10. Your rights and withdrawal

You have the right to know what data is processed, to obtain it, to demand its correction, blocking and destruction, and to withdraw the consent.

Withdrawing this consent means deleting the account: the platform cannot work without processing data. The Operator stops processing and destroys the data within 15 business days. If you have outstanding obligations (unfinished deals, frozen funds, sole ownership of an organization), the Operator gives a reasoned refusal and shows what needs to be done — the reasons are visible before you confirm the deletion.

## 11. Other information

The Operator protects the data: encryption of sensitive fields, access control, action logs, transfer over protected channels. The Operator notifies the authorised body of a personal data security breach within one business day and then the affected users. See the "Personal Data Processing Policy" for details.
