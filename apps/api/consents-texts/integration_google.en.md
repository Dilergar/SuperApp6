# Consent to the Transfer of Data to Google Calendar

Given under Articles 8 and 16 of the Law of the Republic of Kazakhstan "On Personal Data and Their Protection". The Operator is {{legalName}}, BIN {{bin}}.

## 1. Recipient

Google LLC, USA — the Google Calendar service.

## 2. What data is transferred

The e-mail address of your Google account; the events of your calendar on the platform: title, time, description, location; the deadlines of your tasks — if their export is on. Events created in Google Calendar are in turn loaded into the platform.

## 3. Purpose

Two-way calendar synchronisation at your request.

## 4. Term and withdrawal

The consent is valid while the connection is active. Disconnecting in the calendar settings or withdrawing the consent in the "My data" section of the profile stops the transfer: the platform revokes its access to your Google account and deletes the stored access keys. Events already transferred to Google remain in your Google account and are managed there.

## 5. Important

Google processes the data it receives under its own rules as an independent operator. Contact: {{privacyEmail}}.
