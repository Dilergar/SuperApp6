# Consent to the Cross-Border Transfer of Personal Data

Given under Article 16 of the Law of the Republic of Kazakhstan "On Personal Data and Their Protection". The Operator is {{legalName}}, BIN {{bin}}, address: {{address}}.

## 1. What is transferred and why

Browser and phone notifications are technically delivered by the services of browser and operating system vendors. Without them a notification cannot reach the device while the platform is closed.

## 2. Basic recipients

| Recipient | Country | Data | Purpose |
|---|---|---|---|
| Google LLC — Firebase Cloud Messaging | USA | device token, notification text, IP address | delivery of notifications to Chrome and Android |
| Mozilla Corporation — Push Service | USA | device token, notification text, IP address | delivery of notifications to Firefox |
| Apple Inc. — Push Notification service | USA | device token, notification text, IP address | delivery of notifications to Safari and Apple devices |
| Microsoft Corporation — Windows Notification Services | USA | device token, notification text, IP address | delivery of notifications to Edge and Windows |

The notification text is encrypted on the way to your device. You can turn such notifications off in the profile settings — the transfer then stops.

## 3. Recipients at your request

These services are not needed for basic operation. Data is transferred to them only after you connect the service yourself and give a separate consent at the moment of connection:

| Recipient | Country | Data | Purpose |
|---|---|---|---|
| Google LLC — Google Calendar | USA | the e-mail address of your Google account, your calendar events | calendar synchronisation |

## 4. Changes to the list

The list of recipients changes only by publishing a new version of this consent. You are notified of a new version in advance; a new recipient does not receive your data until you accept the version that names it.

## 5. Term and withdrawal

The consent is valid for as long as the account exists. Because the platform cannot inform you of important events without the delivery services, this consent can be withdrawn together with deleting the account; separately, you can turn the notifications themselves off in the settings.

## 6. Where the database is stored

The database containing personal data is stored on servers in the Republic of Kazakhstan (Article 12 of the Law). Contact: {{privacyEmail}}.
