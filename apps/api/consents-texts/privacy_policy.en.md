# Personal Data Processing Policy

Approved by the Operator under subparagraph 1-1) of paragraph 2 of Article 25 of the Law of the Republic of Kazakhstan "On Personal Data and Their Protection".

## 1. Operator and responsible person

The Operator is {{legalName}}, BIN {{bin}}, address: {{address}}. The person responsible for organising the processing of personal data is the {{dpoTitle}} of the Operator. Requests of data subjects: {{privacyEmail}}.

## 2. Principles

- data is processed only for the stated purposes and to the necessary extent;
- the basis for processing is the consent of the data subject or a direct requirement of the law;
- the database containing personal data is stored in the territory of the Republic of Kazakhstan;
- data is kept no longer than the purpose requires and is then destroyed or anonymised;
- access is given only to those who need it for their work, and every access is logged.

## 3. List of personal data

Identification: phone number, surname, first name, patronymic, date of birth, photo. Additional, at the user's wish: city, "about me", e-mail, marital status, links to social networks. Details for contracts and employment, at the user's wish: IIN, residential address, identity document details. User content: messages, tasks, notes, files, documents. Technical: IP address, device details, session identifiers, notification tokens. Biometric data is not processed.

## 4. Roles of the Operator

For users' data the Operator is the owner of the database and the operator. For data that an organization enters into the platform (employees, counterparties, customers), the organization is the owner and the Operator processes the data on its instruction — under the "Data Processing Agreement".

## 5. Protection measures

- encryption of personal data in the database with keys managed by a dedicated engine; every person and every organization has its own key;
- blind indexes instead of plain values where search is needed;
- access control: access to an object is determined by the person's relation to it;
- a separate contour for the Operator's staff: sign-in with SMS confirmation, re-confirmation of dangerous actions, the "four eyes" rule, an immutable log;
- logs of actions with personal data, including the log of data disclosure to staff;
- data transfer only over protected channels;
- a register of transfers to third parties, cross-border transfers and dissemination;
- backups and integrity control.

## 6. Data transfer

The list of recipients is given in the "Consent to the Collection and Processing of Personal Data" and the "Consent to the Cross-Border Transfer". Every actual transfer is recorded; the user sees their own records in the "My data" section.

## 7. Retention

Account data — until the account is deleted. After a deletion request the account is hidden for 14 days (it can be restored), then the data is destroyed or anonymised. Only information with a statutory retention period is kept longer: records of financial operations, signed documents, proofs of the consents given, information about violations of the rules — with restricted access.

## 8. Rights of the data subject

To know whether and what data is held; to obtain it; to demand modification, blocking and destruction; to withdraw consent; to challenge the Operator's actions before the authorised body or a court. Requests are fulfilled within the periods set by the Law.

## 9. Security breach

Having discovered a personal data security breach, the Operator notifies the authorised body within one business day and then the affected data subjects, stating what happened, which data is affected and what should be done. Incidents are kept in a separate register.
